More than photographs

Real love. Meaningful memories. Stories worth sharing.

The Visionmoment Love Story Series is a special feature created to celebrate couples whose weddings we have had the privilege of photographing.

We want to look beyond the wedding-day highlights and hear the personal stories behind them: how the relationship began, what the day meant to you, and how it feels to revisit those memories through your photographs.

Each feature is an opportunity to honour your journey and inspire other couples preparing for their own special day.

What we’ll talk about

Your story, told in your own words.

Every couple is different. The conversation will be relaxed, respectful, and shaped around your experience.

01 / YOUR JOURNEY

How it began

How you met, how your relationship grew, and the moments that made your story special.

02 / YOUR WEDDING

The day you’ll remember

Your favourite memories, the emotions, the details, and the moments you might have missed.

03 / YOUR EXPERIENCE

Behind the photographs

What it was like working with Visionmoment and how you feel seeing your memories preserved.

Participation is by invitation and mutual agreement. We’ll discuss the format and any sharing preferences with you before publishing your feature.

Help another story begin

Know a couple getting married?

If you know someone preparing for their wedding, we’d be grateful if you introduced them to Visionmoment for their photography.

Every referral helps more couples discover our work and gives us the chance to preserve more meaningful memories. When a referred couple books with us, they may also have the opportunity to be considered for a future Love Story feature.

There’s no pressure—just an invitation to help us celebrate more stories of love.
